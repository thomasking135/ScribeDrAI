Understanding the Metrics

Precision

Of the sections assigned to a category by the model, precision measures the proportion that were correctly classified.

Recall

Of the sections that genuinely belong to a category, recall measures the proportion successfully identified by the model.

F1-score

F1-score combines precision and recall into a single measure, helping evaluate the balance between the two.

Support

Support is the number of test examples belonging to each documentation category.

Experimental Design

Clinical sections were derived from the MTSamples medical transcription dataset and classified into five predefined documentation categories.

Source-level grouping was used during the train/test split so that sections from the same original transcription did not appear in both sets. This reduces the risk of information leakage between training and evaluation data.

The experiment compares TF-IDF with Logistic Regression, TF-IDF with a linear Support Vector Machine, and a fine-tuned Bio_ClinicalBERT transformer model.
